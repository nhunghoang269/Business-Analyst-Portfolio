# Adventure Works Sales Dashboard

## Business Problem

Business leaders require a consolidated view of sales performance across regions, product categories and time periods to monitor growth, profitability and target achievement.

## Objective

Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard that enables stakeholders to track key sales KPIs, analyse performance trends and identify high-performing regions and product categories.

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- DAX
- Data Modelling
- Data Visualisation
- KPI Reporting

## Methodology

1. Imported and cleaned Adventure Works sales data
2. Built relationships between fact and dimension tables
3. Created DAX measures for sales, margin and target performance
4. Designed interactive dashboard visuals and filters
5. Analysed trends across years, continents and product categories
6. Presented business insights through KPI and performance dashboards

## Key Outputs

- Sales KPI Dashboard
- Target vs Actual Performance Tracking
- Sales Trend Analysis
- Geographic Sales Comparison
- Product Category Margin Analysis
- Interactive Filters and Drill-Down Capability

## Business Value

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor business performance in real time, identify growth opportunities, compare regional sales performance and support data-driven decision-making.
