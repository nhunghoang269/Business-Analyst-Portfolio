# Data & Business Analytics Portfolio

Welcome to my portfolio! I am an MSc Business Analytics and Artificial Intelligence student in the UK with hands-on experience in business analysis, marketing analytics, digital transformation and data-driven decision-making.

My portfolio showcases projects across descriptive, predictive & prescriptive analytics, recommender systems, database design, SQL, optimisation and simulation. Each project focuses not only on technical implementation, but also on business problem framing, methodology, analytical outputs and practical recommendations for decision-making.

## Portfolio Projects

## Descriptive Analytics and AI

### [Customer Churn Analytics & AI](./Descriptive-Analytics-and-AI/Customer-Churn-Analytics-AI)
This project applies business analytics and AI principles to customer churn management for a subscription-based business. It explores churn drivers, identifies high-risk customers and provides retention recommendations based on analytical findings.

**Skills:** Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn, EDA, Predictive Modelling, Evaluation Metrics, Business Recommendations

### [Movie Recommender System](./Predictive-Analytics-and-AI/Movie-Recommender-System)
This project develops and evaluates recommendation approaches for a movie streaming platform, focusing on personalisation, content discovery, engagement and ethical recommendation design.

**Skills:** Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn, Recommendation Modelling, Predictive Modelling, Evaluation Metrics

## Database Design and SQL

## Prescriptive Analytics and Simulation
