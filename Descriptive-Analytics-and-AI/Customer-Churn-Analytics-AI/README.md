# Customer Churn Analytics & AI

## Business Problem

Subscription-based companies need to understand why customers leave, which customers are most at risk, and what actions can improve retention and business performance.

## Objective

Explore customer behaviour data, identify churn drivers and build a predictive analytics workflow to support proactive customer retention decisions.

## Tools Used

* Python
* Pandas
* Scikit-learn
* Exploratory Data Analysis
* Predictive Modelling
* Evaluation Metrics

## Methodology

1. Defined the churn problem from a business and customer retention perspective
2. Explored customer data including tenure, monthly charges, contract type, support tickets, service usage and satisfaction score
3. Compared churners and non-churners to identify behavioural patterns and risk factors
4. Prepared the data through cleaning, encoding and feature selection
5. Built predictive models to identify customers with higher churn likelihood
6. Evaluated model performance using appropriate metrics
7. Translated analytical findings into retention recommendations

## Key Outputs

* Customer churn analysis workflow
* Churn driver identification
* Predictive model for churn risk
* Model evaluation summary
* Business recommendations for customer retention

## Business Value

The project demonstrates how business analytics and AI can help organisations move from reactive churn reporting to proactive churn prevention. The insights can support targeted retention campaigns, customer support improvements and more evidence-based decision-making.
